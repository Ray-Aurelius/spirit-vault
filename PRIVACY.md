# Spirit Vault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

## The short version
Spirit Vault collects **no personal data**. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no subscriptions, and it never will. You pay once and everything is included.

## The app can't go online
Spirit Vault has **no internet permission**, so it has no way to send anything anywhere. You can check this yourself: Android lists an app's permissions on its Google Play page and under Settings → Apps on your phone.

## What stays on your phone
Your case files (including the case brief, the timestamped notes and findings you type, the logged events from each session, the EMF trace used for session playback, and temperature, humidity and pressure readings from the phone's own sensors where it has them), EVP recordings (including motion-trap sound clips), photos and videos are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and **encrypted** (AES-256) with a key held in your phone's secure hardware. A copy taken off the phone can't be opened. Settings, word-bank choices and blind-review notes and grades are stored there too. If you add the home-screen widget, it shows your open or last case's name and event count; when the app lock is on it shows only "Case open" or "Last case", never the name. The EMF meter and radar widgets read the phone's sensors only while you have switched them on from the widget (a notification shows while they run). What they pick up (EMF changes, movement, recordings) is filed into your open case, or a new untitled one, and stored encrypted on the phone like everything else. While a recording or video is in progress it is held briefly in the app's private storage until it is encrypted. Uninstalling the app deletes everything except copies you exported to your Gallery and backup files.

You can lock the app, or just the Vault, with a PIN. Only a salted, slowed-down fingerprint of the PIN is stored, never the PIN itself. Settings › Privacy dashboard shows what the app can reach and what it has stored, can hide the app from the recent-apps screen and block screenshots, and has a **Delete everything** button.

## Fingerprint, face and phone lock
You can choose to unlock Spirit Vault with your phone's own lock. When you do, **Android** shows its own prompt and checks your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password on your phone, in its secure hardware. Spirit Vault only receives a yes or a no. It **never sees, stores or sends** your fingerprint, face or phone code, and it has no internet access to send them with. This is off unless you turn it on.

## Location and device details in photos
Photos and videos often carry hidden details such as the GPS location they were taken at and the phone's make, model and serial number. Spirit Vault removes these from everything you bring in from your phone and from every copy you export or share. Photos and videos taken in the app never include your location.

## When something leaves your phone
Only when you choose to:
- **Sharing an EVP recording, photo, video or case report** hands a copy to the app you choose.
- **Exporting a photo or video** saves a normal, unencrypted copy to Pictures/Spirit Vault or Movies/Spirit Vault so your Gallery can show it.
- **Backups** are off unless you turn them on. When on, the app writes a password-encrypted backup (AES-256) to Documents/Spirit Vault/Backups on your phone and keeps the two newest. Without your backup password nobody can open it, including us, and the password can't be recovered. Backups are skipped if your phone is low on space.
- **Exported reports and case files** are saved only when you choose to export them, to Documents/Spirit Vault/Case Files on your phone or to a folder you pick in Settings. They stay on your phone unless you share them yourself.
- **Sharing an evidence card** hands an image of one moment (the reading, what was logged, the nearest photo and the EMF trace) to the app you choose. The image carries no location or phone details.
- **Sending a case to a teammate** writes a password-encrypted case file (AES-256) with that case's events, notes, brief and findings, EMF trace and the name you choose to sign it with, if any, and hands it to the app you pick (for example Nearby Share or Bluetooth). Photos and videos are never included. It goes straight from you to them; it never passes through us.
- **Sending feedback** (Settings › Help & feedback) opens your own email app with a message to spiritvaultsupport@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app, and you can read and change it first. We see only what you write and your email address, and use them only to reply.
- **Rating the app** opens its Google Play page. The app never learns whether or how you rated it.
- **Adding pictures from your phone** uses Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the photos and videos you select. They are copied into the encrypted vault; the originals stay where they were.

We never see any of it.

## Permissions and why they're needed
| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Microphone | The radar's sound meter, the Spirit Box listener, the EVP recorder and video sound, only while you have them running |
| Camera | The Spectral Camera, its videos and the flashlight |
| Vibration | The EMF alarm and vibration alerts |
| Notifications (Android 13 and later) | The notice shown while a recording or motion trap keeps running with the screen off, and alerts for EMF spikes, trap triggers and recorded voices. A paired smartwatch shows the same notifications. Only if you allow it |
| Run in the background (foreground service, keep awake) | Keeps an EVP recording, motion trap or the tools you are running going with the screen off (if you turn that on in Settings), and runs the EMF, radar and EVP home-screen widgets you switch on, always with a visible notice and a Stop button |
| Biometrics (fingerprint, face) | Only if you turn on unlocking with your phone's lock. Android does the check; the app only learns yes or no |
| Storage (Android 8 and 9 only) | Exporting photos and videos to your Gallery, and writing backups |

Motion and magnetic sensors need no permission.

## Your phone's own speech services
The Spirit Box listener uses your phone's built-in speech recognition, and Spirit Voice uses its text-to-speech. These are separate apps provided by your phone's maker or Google. Spirit Vault only uses voices installed on the phone. Depending on your phone, its speech recognition may process audio online under that provider's own privacy policy. Spirit Vault never receives that audio.

## Crash reports
If the app closes unexpectedly, it offers to send a crash report the next time you open it. **Nothing is sent unless you tap Send**, and you can read the full report first. A report contains only:
- the app version
- the Android version
- the phone model
- the date
- the location in the app's code where the problem happened

Error messages are left out, because they can contain text from the app. Before a report is shown to you or sent, the app checks it line by line and drops anything that isn't one of the items above. It never includes your case files, recordings, photos, words, names, notes, settings, file names, location or account details. The attachment is named by date only. Reports are encrypted so only the developer can read them, and they are sent through an app you choose, such as email. If you send a report by email we will see your email address, which we use only to reply if you ask. We delete reports once the problem is fixed.

## Purchases
Spirit Vault is bought through Google Play, which handles payment under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive your payment details.

## Children
Spirit Vault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here before the app update that brings it. The commitments to no ads, no subscriptions and no data collection will not change.
