1. For entertainment

Spirit Vault is an entertainment app. It turns your phone's own sensors (magnetic field, motion, microphone, camera and light) into readings, sounds, words and pictures. It can't detect ghosts, spirits or anything paranormal, and nothing it shows is proof of anything. Spirit Voice, Spirit Box and Spirit Board responses are generated by the app.

2. Stay safe and respectful

Use common sense on investigations. Get permission before entering private property, don't enter unsafe or abandoned buildings, take care moving around in the dark, and never use the app while driving. You are responsible for where and how you use it.

3. Not a measuring instrument

Readings come from consumer phone sensors and are not calibrated. Don't rely on them for safety, electrical, medical, legal or any other real-world decisions.

4. Your privacy

Everything you record stays on your phone, encrypted. Spirit Vault has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we collect no personal data. The Privacy Policy explains it in full.
